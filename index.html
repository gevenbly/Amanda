Meet Amanda

Stitching a More Sustainable Wardrobe.

Amanda is an avid sewist and lover of all things creative and sustainable. Her sewing journey began with a simple machine and a vision to upcycle thrifted clothing. As her confidence grew, she began sewing her own clothes with the goal of crafting an entirely handmade wardrobe.

Her projects give discarded and deadstock fabrics—materials otherwise destined for landfill—a new life. For Amanda, sewing is a way to create beauty, reduce textile waste, and build a more thoughtful relationship with what we wear. It’s her passion to share that love of sewing with the wider community.

  • Former middle-grade teacher
  • Toddler mom and passionate sewist
  • Sewing instructor at Remainders Creative Reuse in Pasadena
